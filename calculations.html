🧮 Mathematical Formulas & Arithmetic Derivations

Formal operating systems metrics, equations, and live step-by-step substitution calculations

1. Turnaround Time (TAT)

Total time elapsed from process arrival to complete termination:

TAT = Completion Time - Arrival Time

Represents the total lifespan of the process within the system.

2. Waiting Time (WT)

Total time spent sitting in ready queues waiting for CPU allocation:

WT = Turnaround Time - Burst Time

For in-flight processes: WT = (T_sim - T_arr) - (Burst - Remaining).

3. Average Waiting Time (AWT)

Arithmetic mean of waiting times across all completed processes:

AWT = (Sum of WT_i) / N_completed

4. Average Turnaround Time (ATT)

Arithmetic mean of turnaround times across all completed processes:

ATT = (Sum of TAT_i) / N_completed

5. CPU Utilization (%)

Percentage of time the CPU was actively executing user workloads:

CPU Utilization = (Total Busy Time / Simulation Time) × 100%

6. System Throughput

Rate of successfully completed tasks per unit of simulation horizon:

Throughput = N_completed / Simulation Time [processes/unit]
